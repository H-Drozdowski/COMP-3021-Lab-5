WHAT IS FIDONET

Before the public internet, thousands of dial-up Bulletin Board Systems formed a grassroots global network. FidoNet, established in 1984 by Tom Jennings, connected these systems by having them call each other overnight to exchange messages and files. Each BBS was a node with a phone number, a sysop, and a region designation. Sysops registered their nodes in a shared directory called the Nodelist, published weekly and distributed across the network. At its peak in the early 1990s, FidoNet connected over 30,000 nodes worldwide. This system is that directory.
